# Rocket Swap 0.41.0

- **The ready check and PvP queue summary is now a card**, right under the game's own box: one line each for specialization (with your class), talents (with your hero talent tree) and gear, with the game's icons and a check mark, warning or cross on each.
- **Gear says what is off**: "complete set", or how many pieces are not the saved ones and which slot, with a reminder to save the set if you swapped on purpose.
- **Compared with your presets**: anything that differs from the matching preset (in the PvP queue, your PvP preset) says what the preset uses, and a **Load** button is right there.
- Wrong gear for the content now shows on the same card instead of a second window.
- The card closes when you answer the ready check, and updates if you swap while it is open.
