# Rocket Swap 0.38.2

- **Druids in Travel Form: the preset is refused, and nothing changes.** In Travel Form the
  game cuts the change of specialization, and the addon went on and switched the gear and the
  appearance anyway. Now the Load button is off while you are in the form, with the reason on
  it, and a macro click gets the red warning in the middle of the screen. Leave the form and
  click again.
- **A specialization that does not change stops the switch.** If the game cuts the change of
  specialization for any other reason, the talents and the gear are left as they were.
