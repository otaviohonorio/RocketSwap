# Rocket Swap 0.27.0

First public release.

- Presets that switch specialization, talents, gear and appearance with one click.
- Warning when your gear doesn't fit the content (PvP gear in PvE, PvE gear in PvP).
- Ready check summary of what you're using. If you swapped a piece without saving the gear set,
  it shows the set name with how many pieces you're wearing, instead of "(none)".
- English and Brazilian Portuguese.
